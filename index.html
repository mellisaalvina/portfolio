How I work

Four habits in every project

1

Start from the business number

Goals are written in absolute figures and worked backwards into the funnel, so every metric has a reason to exist.

2

Read the median, not just the mean

Blended results hide outliers. I break data down by day, ad set and post before concluding anything.

3

Test tracking before spending

GTM, Pixel and GA4 are checked before money goes out, and platform numbers are cross-checked.

4

Use AI, then verify it

AI speeds up drafts, code and research; every number, claim and character count is checked by hand.
